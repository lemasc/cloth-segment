# Handoff — M6 Accessory class check (lemasc)

## In plain words
Ran five checks on `accessory`: DFMM eyeglass masks, per-dataset counts, 11- vs 12-class mIoU, and (on the GPU) recall per raw class and a look at LIP sunglasses predicted as `headwear`.
The eyeglass labels look clean. DFMM has few `accessory` images (455 train, 57 val). The retrain lost sunglasses/eyeglass, and scarf is not learned in either run. LIP sunglasses go to `headwear` mostly when a hat or helmet is present.
Full numbers are in `docs/reports/2026-11-05-M6-accessory-check.md`. Nothing was trained and no label or eval code changed.

## Summary
- New analysis code, no change to config, labels or eval: `src/cloth_segment/rawclass.py` (helpers), `scripts/accessory_by_raw_class.py` (step 4), `scripts/lip_sunglasses_headwear.py` (step 5), tests in `tests/test_rawclass.py`.
- Steps 4 and 5 ran on the GPU with the lock held, on both U-Net runs (a few minutes each). They only read `runs/` and `data/`. Results are in `docs/reports/2026-11-05-M6-accessory-by-raw-class.json` and `docs/reports/2026-11-05-M6-lip-sunglasses-headwear.json`.
- Sections 2 and 3 of the report can be pasted into the T6 retrain report (PR #14, someone else's branch, so I did not edit it).

## Decisions and why
- New row `M6` in `PLAN.md` (T1–T9 are not a fit for a check across tasks), as for M3–M5.
- Images are not committed because of dataset licences: the DFMM mask montages (step 1) and the step 5 montages. The step 5 per-image CSVs are not committed either; the summary JSON is, and the script regenerates both.

## Results / findings
See the report. Key numbers: retrain recall on sunglasses/eyeglass is about 0% (original 24–41%); scarf recall is 0–4% in both runs; 85% of the retrain's mostly-`headwear` LIP sunglasses images have a hat in the ground truth. Counts come from `data/splits/splits-v2/manifest.csv`; mIoU from the `metrics.json` files of runs `20261105-0323-7b1798b-unet-r34` and `20261105-1307-1acb88f-unet-r34`.
