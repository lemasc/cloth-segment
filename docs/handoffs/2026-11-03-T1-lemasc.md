## Label findings (from the sample viewer)

1. **DFMM 6 "leggings" are not trousers.** Almost all are knee/thigh-high
   socks or tights, usually under skirts or dresses (377 images). The draft
   mapped them to *pants*; they match LIP/CIHP *socks* (CIHP socks include
   knee-high socks). → map to the same class as socks.
2. **Q6 socks:** consistent across all three datasets (ankle to knee-high
   socks). Rare (0.03–0.16% of pixels, 7–13% of images). Keep socks (+ DFMM
   leggings) → **footwear**; a separate class would be too small to learn.
3. **Q7 belt:** DFMM belts are clean and clear (717 images), but LIP/CIHP
   have no belt class, so the same pixels are pants/upper/dress there. One
   DFMM "belt" is a sweater tied round the waist. → map DFMM belt to
   **ignore (255)**, like bags. Mapping it to accessory would teach a label
   that two of three datasets contradict.
4. **LIP 10 jumpsuits:** wetsuits, baseball uniforms, onesies, full-body
   costumes (690 images). DFMM 21 rompers = jumpsuits, rompers, bodysuits
   (736). Both → *dress / one-piece*, as drafted. CIHP has no such class
   (its jumpsuits are labelled by part), a small conflict to note as a
   limitation.
5. **CIHP 10 torso-skin** → *skin*, as drafted (in 97% of images).
6. **DFMM jewellery** (ring in 65% of images, wrist wear 45%, necklace 32%,
   earrings 12%) is tiny (≤0.03% of pixels each) and has no LIP/CIHP
   counterpart → **ignore**, as drafted.
7. **DFMM 9 neckwear** (48 images): scarves are real, but 21 of 48 images
   have < 50 px of it, which are stray pixels on bracelets and necklaces.
   Harmless as *accessory*; note as annotation noise. DFMM 19 gloves: **0
   images**. DFMM 23 tie: 12 images.

Proposed changes to the kickoff draft table: DFMM 6 → footwear (was pants),
DFMM 10 → ignore (was accessory). Everything else stays.

## DFMM outfits and attributes

- 12,701 masked images = **6,902 outfits** (`id_xxxxxxxx`). 30 outfit IDs
  appear under two categories (e.g. `MEN-Shirts_Polos` and `MEN-Sweaters`):
  the same product listed twice. Group the split by the **bare ID**, not
  category + ID.
- Attribute coverage on masked images: fabric/pattern 12,701 (all), shape
  12,694 (7 missing).
- NA rates on masked images: sleeve 0.2%, lower length 0.5%, upper fabric
  and pattern 0.7%, **outer fabric and pattern 80%** (NA = no outer garment).

E1 candidates (Q1), from the distributions:

| Head | Classes | Balance | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sleeve length | sleeveless 5,287 / short 2,477 / medium 749 / long 4,152 | good | drop "not long" (6) as ignore |
| Lower length | three-point 4,771 / medium-short 1,027 / three-quarter 540 / long 6,292 | ok | |
| Upper pattern | pure 7,095 / graphic 3,512 / striped 765 / floral 463 / others | skewed | |
| Upper fabric | cotton 10,370 (82%) / chiffon 1,467 / knitted 568 / others | heavily skewed | weak choice |

Recommendation: sleeve length + lower length (+ upper pattern), with NA
masked out of the loss (`ignore_index`). They relate directly to garment
masks, which helps explain multi-task results.
